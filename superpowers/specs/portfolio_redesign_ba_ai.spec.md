---

## 1. Information Architecture & Navigation Overhaul
### Problem:
Current navbar has 8 competing tabs ("Summary", "Skills", "Experience", "Trade Life Cycle", "Case Studies", "BA Framework", "Contact", "Resume"). Everything screams at the user. Page is ~6,000px long.

### Solution:
Prune down to a streamlined 4-anchor funnel:
1. `#work` — **Featured BA + AI Projects** (Top 3 flagship systems)
2. `#lifecycle` — **Interactive Trade Lifecycle** (Live clearing & settlement simulator)
3. `#skills` — **BA + AI Capability Matrix** (AI prompt engineering, domain, requirements, tech)
4. `#contact` — **Executive Connect** (Direct contact + ATS Resume download)

---

## 2. Content Standard: ASD-STE-100 (Simplified Technical English)
- **Constraint 1:** Sentences under 20 words.
- **Constraint 2:** Strict active voice (Built, Mapped, Shipped, Automated, Verified).
- **Constraint 3:** Zero AI buzzword fluff (no "orchestrated paradigms", no "synergized holistic methodologies").
- **Constraint 4:** Measurable business metrics attached to every technical achievement.
