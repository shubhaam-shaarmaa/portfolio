# Spec & Scratchpad: Portfolio Modernization & BA+AI Re-Architecture

> **Status:** `Phase 1: Adversarial 3-Persona Critique`  
> **Goal:** Transform the bloated, text-heavy, legacy BA portfolio into an ultra-modern, high-impact **BA + AI** showcase with executive visual hierarchy and ASD-STE-100 clarity.  
> **Target Outcome:** Recruiter/Hiring Manager 10-second hook: *"Woah, I need to hire this guy!"*

---

## 1. Problem Statement & Diagnosis
- **Length & Fatigue:** Infinite scrolling with 10 case studies, massive embedded BRD/FRD markdown tables, and excessive lists.
- **Tone & Fluff:** Obvious synthetic AI-generated corporate buzzwords ("orchestrated paradigms", "facilitated comprehensive synergies").
- **Missing AI Positioning:** Currently reads like a 2018 traditional financial BA. Zero showcase of modern AI (Agentic workflows, LLM requirements generation, AI compliance checking, RAG pipelines, prompt architecture).
- **Broken Visual Hierarchy:** 8 competing nav items, dozens of gold badges, competing cards with no primary focal point.
- **Language Standard:** Needs ASD-STE-100 (Simplified Technical English) — concise verbs, active voice, <20 words per sentence, explicit metrics.

---

## 2. Critique Personas (Adversarial Breakdown)
- **Persona 1: The Tier-1 FinTech/AI Hiring Manager & Technical Recruiter**
  - *Focus:* 10-second resume/portfolio scan, signal-to-noise ratio, AI credibility, hireability factor.
- **Persona 2: The Principal Product Designer / UX Architect**
  - *Focus:* Cognitive load, typography, layout pacing, progressive disclosure, modern FinTech SaaS aesthetics (Stripe/Linear/Vercel level).
- **Persona 3: The Lead Systems Architect & ASD-STE-100 Technical Editor**
  - *Focus:* Content pruning, stripping AI fluff, technical authenticity, ASD-STE-100 compliance, eliminating competing elements.
